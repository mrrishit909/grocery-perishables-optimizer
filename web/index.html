Grocery Perishables Optimizer

An invented chain of twelve stores and twenty perishable items. Demand read through stockouts, shelf life read from lots and their temperature logs, a strawberry overstock marked down by dynamic programming, and orders for a heatwave week, every decision scored against the simulation truth. …
The chain, its sales, lots, temperature logs, weather and shelf camera are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real retailer or real data. The truth is shown only to judge the models, which never see it. No image model runs: the shelf camera's counts are simulated. No outside model or service is called.
